Ohutum autosõit

Safer Driving

Educational · Estonian Transport Administration

An educational game made for the Estonian Transport Administration. The player finds a seat for the driver and the passenger, adjusts seatbelts and headrests, then adds a dog and the luggage and fastens everything securely.

02

Flatten the Curve

Society simulation · Ideal 2D gas

A game of managing a society that is fighting a deadly virus. The society is modelled as an ideal 2D gas. The player has to keep the infrastructure for education, business, recreation, and healthcare intact. Opening the borders to exchange students, immigrant workers, or tourists brings benefits, but may also introduce the virus.

Windows build (opens in a new tab)

Kratt

GameMaker Studio · Stealth horror

A stealthy horror game made with GameMaker Studio. As rain falls, particles turn into other particles, and drawing with blend modes makes the lanterns glow. Made in a team of two with Rasmus Rikken. I wrote the code, except for the menus, and some of the pixel art.

Found Guilty

GameMaker Studio · Dialogue engine

A serious text-based crime investigation game made with GameMaker Studio. A self-made dialogue engine reads a JSON file and keeps track of game state. I worked in a team of four with Laura Einas, Tuuli Maiste, and Endrico Mihelman, doing the coding and the essential story editing.

Svaneti, Georgia

A game of keeping your balance in the picturesque county of Svaneti, Georgia. Put one foot in front of the other while avoiding rocks, lizards, and other obstacles. Water, wine, and khachapuri provide energy and consolation.

06

Cheap Sheep

GameMaker Studio · Box2D

A sheep shearing game made with GameMaker Studio. The Box2D physics engine makes the sheep react realistically. The player has to be quick and precise, because the sheep will bleed if cut in. As they earn money, they can buy more sheep and upgrades, and level their sheep up.

GameMaker Studio

A dynamic maze game made with GameMaker Studio. The enemies use a sophisticated approach to AI: they track the player while staying dispersed. Tiles get replaced with other tiles, and deactivated enemies get a shader applied to them.
